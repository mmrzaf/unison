# Public Website

The Public Website communicates Waiotech publicly in English and Persian, supports discoverability, and contains only governed public-safe content. It is not an authenticated product surface or documentation authority.

## The Public Website

Waiotech must use the Public Website for public understanding and access, not Tenant operation.

The Public Website is Waiotech's public information, trust, discovery, support-entry, and product-entry surface.

It explains Waiotech to prospective customers, partners, applicants, procurement reviewers, security reviewers, and users seeking public information.

## Maintain English and Persian as equal public content editions

Waiotech must maintain English and Persian as equal public content editions.

The Public Website provides complete English and Persian editions identified by `en` and `fa`.

Persian is the default public edition at `/`. `/fa/` remains a stable Persian alias, while English is served under `/en/`. The root path renders Persian content directly rather than presenting a language-choice page.

Both editions must communicate equivalent product meaning, security claims, legal information, support information, and calls to action. One language must not contain materially stronger promises, missing limitations, or incomplete safety information.

## Publish truthful public content that explains the governed Waiotech product

Waiotech must publish truthful public content that explains the governed Waiotech product.

Public Website content may include:

- product purpose and capabilities;
- Plant Model, Process Operations, Maintenance, Inventory, and Reliability scope;
- industry and use-case explanations;
- security, privacy, reliability, accessibility, and recovery information;
- public contact, sales, support, legal, and policy pages;
- public-safe articles and guides;
- Android Work App distribution links;
- Tenant Dashboard sign-in entry;
- a link to a separately governed service-status capability.

Every product statement must remain consistent with Product Authority.

## Keep the Public Website outside authenticated product operation and authority

Waiotech must keep the Public Website outside authenticated product operation and authority.

The Public Website must not:

- expose Tenant records;
- perform Work Order, Inventory, IAM, policy, publication, or governance actions;
- expose Platform Admin;
- grant product authority;
- implement an alternate product workflow;
- present marketing language as normative authority;
- publish protected documentation or Tenant evidence;
- make unsupported capability, security, performance, or compliance claims.

## Treat the Public Website as explanatory content derived from authority

Waiotech must treat the Public Website as explanatory content derived from authority.

Public pages may explain Waiotech and may present public-safe guidance, but Product Authority and Engineering Authority remain controlling. Public content must be corrected when it diverges from authority.

## Guarantee high-quality public discoverability controls without promising external ranking

Guarantee high-quality public discoverability controls without promising external ranking.

Public content must be technically discoverable, accessible, stable, and understandable in both supported languages.

Waiotech must provide meaningful page identity, navigable information architecture, durable public URLs, appropriate public metadata, and content that matches the subject represented by each page.

Search-engine placement is not a product guarantee because ranking is controlled by external systems.
